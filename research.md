The 'Myrmecia pyriformis' or the 'bulldog ant' 💀🥶

This ant is the most dangerous because their venom triggers severe allergic reactions and anaphylaxis, and they have been responsible for multiple recorded human fatalities in Australia.
And also because they use their long, serrated mandibles to grab a victim while curling their abdomen to thrust a barbless stinger repeatedly. 
And because she have a aggressive behavior: 
They show little fear of humans, will aggressively defend their nests, and are known to chase intruders over long distances.
